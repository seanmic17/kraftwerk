prompt.ipynb houses my research and findings about the willingness of customers who were surveyed about coupons received for bars, coffee shops, restaurants in close proximity to their drives.
Many conditions were taken into account including age, passenger type, and spend.

My last research involved investigating those drivers with and without kids, all other variables constant (age, frequency of visit), and the variable was shifted from bars and restaurants to Coffee shops.
An inverse relationship was discovered; drivers with kis were less apt to stop at coffee shops when served coupons than those drivers without kids.
